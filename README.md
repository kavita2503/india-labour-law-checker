# India Labour Law Compliance Checker

A free, open-source tool to check HR policies, offer letters, and job descriptions against key Indian labour laws.

No login. No API key. Works entirely in the browser.

## What it checks

- **POSH Act 2013** — ICC constitution, sexual harassment policy
- **Maternity Benefit Act 1961** — 26-week entitlement, creche facilities
- **EPF & Gratuity** — mandatory contribution and eligibility clauses
- **ESIC** — coverage and contribution requirements
- **Code on Wages 2019** — minimum wage compliance
- **Industrial Disputes Act** — flags at-will employment and no-notice termination
- **Non-compete clauses** — largely unenforceable in India under Contract Act
- **DPDP Act 2023** — employee data privacy obligations
- **Working hours** — statutory limits under Factories Act
- **Training bonds, probation, leave entitlements, overtime**
